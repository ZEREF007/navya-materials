# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Allo** | Himalayan nettle (*Girardinia diversifolia*). Hand-processed fiber traditional to mountain communities. |
| **Bast fiber** | Fiber extracted from the inner bark of a plant stem (banana, hemp, allo all bast-type). |
| **Decortication** | Mechanical removal of woody/bark material from a fiber-bearing stem. |
| **Retting** | Controlled microbial/water process to loosen fiber from surrounding tissue. |
| **Pseudo-stem** | Banana plant's apparent trunk — actually tightly rolled leaf sheaths. Source of banana fiber. |
| **Bio-composite** | Material combining natural fibers with bio-based resins/binders. |
| **MOC** | "Map of Content" — Obsidian convention for an index page. |
| **ADR** | "Architecture Decision Record" — short doc capturing one design decision + rationale. |
